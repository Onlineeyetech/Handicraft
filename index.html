The Atelier

Fire, Breath
& Patience

01
The Gathering

Molten silica is drawn from a furnace burning at 1,300°C. The maestro reads the glass like a musician reads silence — its weight, viscosity, and temperament determined by decades of intuition.

02
The Breath

A single exhalation into the blowpipe begins the form. The glass remembers every breath, every rotation, every second of contact with the cold night air. Time and temperature become one.

03
The Annealing

Every piece rests in a cooling kiln for 14 hours, releasing internal stresses within its crystalline structure. Rushed cooling creates fractures — only patience creates permanence.

04
The Certification

A laser-etched provenance mark, a numbered certificate, and a documented lineage connecting each piece to its maker — ensuring your acquisition is historically anchored.

Maestro Vetraio · Lead Artisan

Third-generation glassblower whose grandfather worked beside the great Seguso. Giuseppe's monumental vessels have entered the permanent collections of eight national museums across Europe.

Color Alchemist · Pigment Master

Trained in Moscow before spending fifteen years perfecting ancient Murano color traditions. Elena's chromatic formulas — involving gold dust and crushed mineral oxide — are closely guarded secrets.

24 yrs
AT
Akira Tanaka
Form Sculptor · Abstract Series

The unlikely fusion of Japanese wabi-sabi philosophy with Venetian glass tradition. Akira's abstract forms occupy the space between accident and intention — shaped by a conversation with the material itself.
